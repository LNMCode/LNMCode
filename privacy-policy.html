Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LMNCode (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile games and applications. Please read it carefully to understand our privacy practices.

Last updated
July 3, 2026
Developer
LMNCode

1. Introduction

Thank you for playing our games. This Privacy Policy applies to LMNCode mobile games and applications that reference or link to this page.

Our games may store gameplay-related information locally on your device so they can provide the gameplay experience and remember your settings. LMNCode does not sell user data and does not share locally stored gameplay data for direct marketing purposes.

2. Information Stored Locally

Our games may save gameplay-related information directly on your device. Depending on the game, this information may include:

  • Game progress and completed levels
  • High scores and gameplay achievements
  • Game and display settings
  • Sound and vibration preferences
  • Other gameplay preferences

The information described above is stored locally and is used to provide the game experience and retain your selected preferences.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay information is used only to support app functionality, maintain game progress, and remember your settings. This helps the game continue from the state and preferences saved on your device.

4. Selling and Sharing

LMNCode does not sell user data. We do not share locally stored gameplay data for direct marketing purposes.

5. Device Permissions

Some games may request access to device features only when that access is needed for gameplay or app functionality. You can review, allow, or withdraw app permissions through your device settings.

Restricting a permission may prevent a feature that depends on that permission from working as intended.

6. Data Retention and Loss

Gameplay information stored locally generally remains on your device while the app and its data remain available. LMNCode does not control your device backups or operating-system data management.

If you delete or reinstall the app, reset your device, or clear the app's data, locally stored game progress and settings may be permanently lost.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices

You control locally stored game data through the app and your device controls. You may also manage device permissions at any time through your operating-system settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date shown above will be revised.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or LMNCode's privacy practices, please contact us using the information below.
