02 Development approach

Play comes first.

Features matter when they improve the player's understanding, control, or enjoyment—not simply because they can be added.

01

Prototype the mechanic

Find the interaction that makes the idea enjoyable before expanding the scope around it.

02

Design for the device

Build controls, layouts, and performance targets around how people actually play on a phone or tablet.

03

Polish useful feedback

Use motion, sound, and visual response to make game state and player actions easy to understand.

03 Developer profile

Design and engineering, developed together.

I work across gameplay design, Unity development, interface behavior, and mobile performance. Keeping these disciplines close makes it easier to test ideas quickly and maintain a consistent player experience from prototype through release.
